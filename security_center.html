Standard: OWASP Top 10 & NIST SP 800-115 Methodological Evaluation

1. Executive Summary

A full-scope penetration test and vulnerability assessment was conducted against the PaySecure e-commerce payment infrastructure. The evaluation assessed 6 critical threat classes spanning network infrastructure, authentication, input handling, database queries, and session management. Following identification and controlled exploitation, targeted remediation controls were engineered and verified via systematic post-mitigation re-testing.
